# Lessons for KOML

The denominator is the domain's central risk: recording only replies makes every market appear successful. A useful market comparison must include eligible contacts who did not respond and use a consistent response window.

The synthetic experiment validated exact comparison arithmetic: one planted market qualifies, all counts reconcile, and removing its responses removes its qualification. It did not validate that Henry has suitable data, will use the tool, or that a buyer will pay. The initial commercial assumption remains unmeasured.

Operating rule: publish exact input definitions and independently computed expectations before tuning thresholds. Keep descriptive response comparisons separate from claims about future demand or savings.
